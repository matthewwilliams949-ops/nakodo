The short version

What Nakodo is

Nakodo captures approved snippets of what you're building, compares them privately across users, and outputs exactly one thing: an introduction to the right person. No feed, no likes, no faces, no performance.

The one-liner it lives next to:

Your agent knows what you're building better than anyone. We make it your networker — a social network with no feed, no faces, and no performance, that only ever outputs one thing: the right person, today.
The name

Nakodo · 仲人

From the Japanese 仲人 (nakōdo) — naka (between) + hito (person): the traditional go-between of an omiai, the arranged introduction. The name was chosen because the go-between's centuries-old job maps almost line-for-line onto the product's trust rules. It isn't a metaphor stretched to fit — the role already is the product.

The nakōdo's roleThe product's guarantee
Studies both sides in private before either has seen the otherThe matcher sees everything; humans see nothing until double opt-in
Presents each party to the other in their best honest lightThe agent as emissary — it pitches your work on your behalf
Carries interest and refusal indirectly, so no one loses faceDeclines are never revealed, in either direction
Arranges the one meeting, then steps backThe only output is an intro — no feed, no browse surface

The go-between's defining trait is discretion — the deliberate opposite of the gossipy Yiddish yenta. Where the yenta performs, the nakōdo is quiet and trusted. That contrast is the anti-LinkedIn, anti-build-in-public stance in a single word.

Availability. nakodo is free on npm; nakodo.dev / .so / .app are open (.com taken, not required). No existing Nakodo MCP server, dev tool, or startup surfaced. One flag for the trademark read: Nakoda (with an a), a common Indian brand name.

Visual identity · the mark

人 — person, in two strokes

The logo is the character 人 itself, in bone white: no accent, no stylization, no ornament. Drawn calligraphically — two curved brush strokes with tapered ends, crossing just below the tip so the left stroke rises above the join.

The crossing is deliberate. Strokes that meet only at their points read as an arrow; strokes that cross read as the character. Balanced in weight, but with brush character — not a rigid mirror. It stays authentic (this is 人 as a brush writes it), and it holds down to favicon size. The logo is also typeable: 人 is U+4EBA, so it renders natively in terminals, READMEs and npm — the banner 人 nakodo needs no image.

Visual identity · palette

Dark-first, one amber

The audience lives in dark terminals, and the product's story is things developing quietly in the dark. So the ground is near-black; bone is the light. One warm colour — amber — is reserved exclusively for the moment an introduction happens. Scarcity is what makes it mean something; it never decorates ordinary UI, and never touches the mark.

Where the brand lives

Most people meet Nakodo as a command, not a logo. The install and first run carry the voice — quiet, terse, honest about the resting state.

Voice & tone

The introvert's tool, written like docs

Dry, discreet, anti-performance. It never sounds like a social network or a growth-hack; it speaks the way a trusted go-between would — understated, confident, allergic to hype.

Say

the right person · today · quietly · in private · an introduction · off the record · your agent knows · no feed, no faces

Don't say

network · grow your audience · build in public · engagement · viral · supercharge · AI-native · 10x · unlock

The guarantees, written like a spec

The four trust rules are the real differentiator, so they get the audience's native idiom for a promise you can hold someone to — RFC 2119 language, in mono.

1. Nothing is captured without per-snippet approval.
2. Profiles MUST NOT be displayed — only compared.
3. No feed exists. The only output is an introduction.
4. Declines MUST NOT be revealed. Ever.
Guiding philosophy

The person in between

The whole brand is the behaviour of a good go-between: it knows more than either party, works behind a curtain, and reveals only what serves the meeting. The Japanese source stays underneath — in the name and the mark — never worn on the surface. A story you reveal, not explain.

Lineage

Sibling to Auno, by method not language

Nakodo is the second Japanese-sourced name in this body of work, after Auno (from aun, the paired breath). The two are unrelated products; the shared thread is the method — name the promise precisely at its source, then wear it lightly. Auno named synchrony; Nakodo names the discreet introduction. Where Auno is warm paper and one breath, Nakodo is dark, quiet, and one bright moment.
