## Adopted now (concierge + launch phase)

1. **Every card carries the mutual-benefit "why."** KIEZ: "AI earns its place by explaining why… that visible reasoning is the trust mechanism," and matching is *mutually beneficial* — each holds a currency the other wants, any axis — never "meet an expert" (extractive) and never ranked/scored. → Operationalized in `concierge-playbook.md`.
2. **Over-invest in the first introduction.** A mediocre first intro kills activation permanently; a brilliant one creates "who's next." Free while matching is manual — hand-pick each user's first card. → Playbook rule #1.
3. **"Being wanted" beats "you have mail."** KIEZ's reverse-check ("someone two streets away needs your perspective — interested?") is the sleeper retention hook. → Card copy frames the intro from the *wanted* side wherever true: "someone building X could use exactly what you have," not just "someone worth meeting."
4. **Demand-side read at onboarding (one input, two uses).** KIEZ's registration asks *what perspective would help you most* as multiple-choice — which doubles as a live map of what supply to recruit. → Concierge logs the needed-perspective category of every ask; the recruit list for the next seed wave comes from that distribution.
5. **Luminary seeding, as an equal.** One respected builder in the MCP scene using Nakodo like any other user — never gated bait, never "celebrity access" — whose organic mention is asymmetric upside. → Identify 3–5 candidates during the r/mcp phase; the public-reply approach applies to them too.
6. **Founder-channel fit is confirmed, and the dogfood story is the narrative.** KIEZ's channel pass ruled out charisma/promoter channels for Matthew and landed on concierge recruiting + build-in-public — exactly Nakodo's plan. The extra nugget: *the founder's own dogfooding as the story* — Matthew is his own ICP (solo builder, no co-founder), so "my agent is finding me my people, watch" is an authentic build-in-public arc for X between launch posts.

## The pre-seed narrative upgrade (why Nakodo survives the graveyard)

KIEZ's case studies name the four things that killed every professional-matching product — and Nakodo has a structural answer to each. This goes in the deck:

| Killer (who it killed) | Nakodo's structural answer |
|---|---|
| **Low frequency** — nothing retains between intros (Lunchclub's fatal flaw, Shapr, Bumble Bizz) | Capture is ambient inside daily agent sessions — frequency is *inherited from the coding workflow*, not manufactured by a ritual |
| **Profile staleness** — nobody updates a profile for an algorithm | Snippets are a byproduct of real work (one input, two uses); the record is proof-of-work, self-refreshing |
| **Match quality decays at scale** (Lunchclub post-pivot) | Verified build-history data beats self-description; concierge until density justifies an algorithm |
| **Recruiting gravity** — consumer networking drifts into hiring (Shapr sold to a recruiter, CoffeeMug pivoted, Bizz died) | Named as a conscious strategic fork, not a drift: collaboration framing defended in all copy; hiring adjacency only ever as a deliberate later decision |

## Guardrails (from the graveyard, now house rules)

- **Never trade the constraint for pool size.** Lunchclub deleted its own location filter to grow the pool and died of blandness. Nakodo's constraint — builders with agents, proof-of-work data — IS the moat. No widening to generic professionals.
- **"Networking" is the cursed verb.** Extractive, fatiguing, stalled four companies. Say *introductions, your person, collaborator, co-founder*. ⚠️ **Open item for Matthew:** the one-liner says "makes it your **networker**" — recommend migrating to "**matchmaker**" (brand-true: nakōdo literally means matchmaker; the r/mcp draft already uses it). His call — it's his coinage.
- **Utility, not a feeling.** Sell "find your co-founder / get the perspective," never "feel less alone."
- **Tailwind honesty.** Lunchclub mistook COVID for durable demand. Our tailwind (agent adoption) is structurally durable, but the gate metrics — not the tailwind — are the evidence.

## Parked for v1.2+ (behind the funnel-metric discipline)

- **Single-player value at N=1** — the waiting-room problem ("the empty Sunday"). KIEZ's answer: make the record genuinely useful alone. Nakodo candidate: a weekly "what you shipped" digest from snippets. Only if seed-phase feedback says the waiting room is killing activation.
- **The keystone invite** — "anyone you'd want in your corner? invite them" adds both people to each other's graph; acquisition + seeding + retention in one mechanic. Strong candidate for the first growth feature after gates pass.
- **Founding-cohort counter** — "founding 100 builders, 64 claimed" on the site (Nextdoor/Timeleft threshold mechanics; honest scarcity only). Test candidate for launch week.
- **Near-you bias → the coffee** — location already collected; when both sides share a city, the revealed intro page can suggest meeting in person. This is the on-ramp to the entire KIEZ local thesis inside Nakodo.
